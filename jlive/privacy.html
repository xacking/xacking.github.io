Privacy Policy – Japan Live

Last updated: 19 October 2026. Developer and publisher: JamsXtudio (contact: verxion@proton.me).

What the app stores

Your favourite channels, saved wallpapers and game records (best scores, wins, puzzles solved) are stored only on your device. There is no account and we do not receive this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

No ads, no tracking

The app shows no ads, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, does not use your advertising identifier and does not track you.

Content downloads

The app downloads its channel list, live-stream list, quizzes, puzzles and wallpapers from GitHub Pages (github.com). These requests contain no personal information beyond what any web request contains (such as your IP address), which GitHub may log.

Videos

Live streams are published by their channels on YouTube and play in YouTube’s embedded player. When you watch, YouTube receives information about your viewing under the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Photos

If you tap “Save to Photos” on a wallpaper, the app asks for permission to add that image to your photo library. It never reads your photos.

Children

The app is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete. Questions: verxion@proton.me.

Independence

Japan Live is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the broadcasters, venues or camera owners whose public YouTube streams it links to. Their names are shown only to credit them.
