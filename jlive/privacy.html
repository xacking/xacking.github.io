Privacy Policy – Japan Live

Last updated: 21 October 2026. Developer and publisher: JamsXtudio (contact: verxion@proton.me).

What the app stores

Your favourite channels, saved wallpapers and game records (best scores, wins, puzzles solved) are stored only on your device. There is no account and we do not receive this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may also fill ad space through its mediation partners Meta Audience Network and Pangle (ByteDance). Ads appear as a banner on some screens and, occasionally, as a full-screen ad after you finish a game. There are no ads on the TV player.

To show and measure ads, these companies may collect your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. They use this under their own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services, Meta Privacy Policy and Pangle Privacy Policy.

Your choices. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change it at any time in Settings › Privacy choices. On iPhone, ads are only personalised if you allow tracking when asked. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s Google settings. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.

The app has no analytics SDK and we never receive your personal information from these companies.

Content downloads

The app downloads its channel list, live-stream list, quizzes, puzzles and wallpapers from GitHub Pages (github.com). These requests contain no personal information beyond what any web request contains (such as your IP address), which GitHub may log.

Videos

Live streams are published by their channels on YouTube and play in YouTube’s embedded player. When you watch, YouTube receives information about your viewing under the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

Radio

Radio stations stream directly from each station’s own server, which receives your IP address like any web request.

Photos

If you tap “Save to Photos” on a wallpaper, the app asks for permission to add that image to your photo library. It never reads your photos.

Children

The app is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access or delete. For data collected by the ad networks, use the choices above or contact them through the policies linked. Questions: verxion@proton.me.

Independence

Japan Live is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the broadcasters, venues or camera owners whose public YouTube streams it links to. Their names are shown only to credit them.
