Privacy Policy – DMV Permit Test 2026

Last updated: 19 October 2026. Developer and publisher: JamsXtudio (contact: verxion@proton.me).

What the app stores

Your chosen state, your answers, progress, streak, marked questions, mock test results and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account and we do not receive this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

No ads, no tracking

The app shows no ads, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, does not use your advertising identifier and does not track you.

Question updates

The app downloads updated question banks from GitHub Pages (github.com). This request contains no personal information beyond what any web request contains (such as your IP address), which GitHub may log.

Children

The app is intended for learner drivers (usually 15 and older) and adults preparing for a written driving test. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete. Questions: verxion@proton.me.

Independence

This app is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any DMV, Secretary of State, Department of Public Safety or other government agency. All questions are written by us from the states' official driver handbooks.
