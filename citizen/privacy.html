Privacy Policy – Citizenship Test Prep 2026

Last updated: 18 October 2026. Developer and publisher: JamsXtudio (contact: verxion@proton.me).

What the app stores

Your chosen test, your answers, progress, streak, marked questions, mock exam results and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account and we do not receive this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you consent, personalise ads. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form; you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone, the app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency); if you decline, ads are not personalised. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Question updates

The app downloads updated question banks from GitHub Pages (github.com). This request contains no personal information beyond what any web request contains (such as your IP address), which GitHub may log.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for citizenship or residency tests and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or delete; for data held by Google, see Google's privacy policy. Questions: verxion@proton.me.

Independence

This app is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any government or official test provider. Official questions are reproduced from public official sources; other questions are written by us from official study material.
